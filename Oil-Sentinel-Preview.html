What these images do — and don't — tell us

The imagery can show the facilities and broad surface changes. It cannot resolve tank fill-height differences with sufficient accuracy to compute weekly barrels. Free NAIP aerial imagery is sharper but typically years apart. This dashboard deliberately reports no unverified satellite storage estimates.

TRADE INTERPRETATION

How the signal is formed

When both national crude and Cushing draw, inventories suggest tighter physical supply. When both build, looser supply. Opposite directions produce Mixed. No chart pattern, EIA consensus or macro surprise is inferred.
